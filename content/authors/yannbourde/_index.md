I studied Electrical Engineering and Information Technology at ETH Zürich, with a focus on machine learning and signal processing applications to the medical domain. Before that, I completed my Bachelor's in Electrical Engineering at McGill University in Montreal. For my Master's thesis, I worked on fine-tuning latent diffusion models to synthesize PET images from CT scans. After graduating, I joined Krauthammer Lab to continue working on machine learning solutions for clinical applications. My current work is focused on machine learning research and software development in the medical setting.
